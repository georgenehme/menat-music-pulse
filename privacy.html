Privacy

Your watchlist and appearance preference are saved in your browser’s local storage. They are not sent to MENAT Music Pulse.

The dashboard does not collect email addresses or use advertising analytics. It is hosted by GitHub Pages, which handles ordinary web request logs. Fonts are requested from Google Fonts; cover artwork is requested from the chart provider. Opening a source or listening link takes you to that provider.

Chart collection runs separately from visitors’ browsers. Only public chart metadata and collection status are delivered to this website.

You can clear your watchlist and preferences by clearing this site’s browser data.
